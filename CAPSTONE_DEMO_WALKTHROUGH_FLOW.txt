🏛️ 1. ANG CORE CONCEPT (ELEVATOR PITCH SA PANEL)
================================================================================
"Magandang araw po sa ating panel. Ang aming system ay isang Financial Management
System (Transaction Core) para sa E-Commerce Marketplace na may AI-Driven
Transaction, Payment, and Financial Reporting.

Lahat ng transaksyon mula sa aming external client o subsystems (E-Commerce,
HRMS, Supply Chain, Fleet) ay dumadaan sa isang 4-Stage Automated Pipeline:
  [1] External Ingestion -> [2] AI Risk Evaluation -> [3] Manager Approval -> [4] GL Posting

Ito ay mahigpit na sumusunod sa:
  - Maker-Checker Segregation of Duties (ang nag-encode ay hindi pwedeng mag-approve)
  - Balanced Double-Entry Bookkeeping (Debit = Credit sa General Ledger)
  - Audit Trail Logging para sa transparency at compliance."

📍 4. HAKBANG 3: M2M API SIMULATOR (PAGPASOK NG TRANSACTIONS)
================================================================================
Dito ipapakita kung paano konektado ang external platforms sa Financial Core:
1. Pumunta sa sidebar -> M2M Simulator.
2. Demo A: Normal Transaction (Low Risk)
   - I-click ang "Online Sales Revenue" (E-Commerce) o "Cement Purchase" (Supply Chain).
   - I-click ang "Send to Core".
   - Makikita sa API Response Log:
     * Status: 201 Created / Processed
     * AI Risk Score: Mababa (e.g. 15/100)
     * AI Flag: NORMAL (Low Risk)
3. Demo B: Suspicious / Fraud Transaction (High Risk Anomaly)
   - I-click ang "SUSPICIOUS Employee Claim" o "OFFSHORE Steel Import".
   - I-click ang "Send to Core".
   - Makikita sa API Response Log:
     * Awtomatikong nag-trigger ang AI Anomaly Detector.
     * AI Flag: CRITICAL / HIGH RISK na may detalyadong AI Audit Reason.

📍 5. HAKBANG 4: APPROVALS & MAKER-CHECKER PIPELINE
================================================================================
1. Mag-login o mag-switch sa Finance Manager (manager01).
2. Pumunta sa sidebar -> Approvals.
3. Ipakita ang 4-Stage Visual Audit Pipeline Banner sa itaas:
   [01 External Txn] -> [02 AI Evaluation] -> [03 Manager Approval] -> [04 GL Posting]
4. Makikita sa pila ang mga transactions na ipinadala mula sa simulator:
   - Normal Transactions: May berdeng "Normal" badge.
   - Flagged Transactions: May dilaw/pulang "AI Flagged / High Risk" badge.
5. Pagsusuri at Aksyon:
   - Click "Approve": Diretso itong maipo-post sa General Ledger.
   - Click "Reject": Magpa-pop up ang Confirmation Dialog para humingi ng
     kumpirmasyon at dahilan bago tanggihan ang transaction.

DEFENSE COMPLIANCE HIGHLIGHT:
- Maker-Checker Rule: Hindi pinapayagan ng system na ang gumawa ng transaksyon
  ang siya ring mag-apruba nito (Segregation of Duties).


================================================================================
📍 6. HAKBANG 5: SUBLEDGERS (AP, AR, CASH, AT DISBURSEMENT)
================================================================================
Ipakita ang operational accounting modules:

1. Accounts Payable (AP):
   - Talaan ng lahat ng bills at bayarin sa suppliers.
   - May Aging Schedule Summary: Current, 1-30 days, 31-60 days, 61-90 days, Over 90 days.
2. Disbursement Module:
   - Ang mga naaprubahang bills ay gagawan ng Disbursement Voucher.
   - Pipiliin ang babawasang bangko (BDO, BPI, UnionBank) at iki-click ang "Disburse".
3. Accounts Receivable (AR) & Collections:
   - Sinusubaybayan ang collectibles at pautang sa mga customer.
   - Kapag nagbayad ang customer, irerecord ito sa Collections module.
4. Cash Management:
   - Real-time balances ng bawat bank account at monitoring ng cash position.


================================================================================
📍 7. HAKBANG 6: GENERAL LEDGER (ANG ACCOUNTING ENGINE)
================================================================================
1. Pumunta sa sidebar -> General Ledger.
2. Double-Entry Journal Entries:
   - Ang bawat approved transaction o disbursement ay awtomatikong may katapat na
     balanseng Journal Entry sa database:
     * Halimbawa: Debit Cash (1010) / Credit Sales Revenue (4010)
     * Halimbawa: Debit Accounts Payable (2010) / Credit Cash in Bank (1010)
3. Precision Formatting:
   - Naka-align sa kanan gamit ang tabular figures (font-mono tabular-nums).
   - Laging balanseng-balanse ang Total Debits at Total Credits.
4. Sub-ledger Navigation:
   - May tabs para sa Journal Entries, AP Ledger, AR Ledger, Cash Ledger,
     at Chart of Accounts (COA).


================================================================================
📍 8. HAKBANG 7: FINANCIAL REPORTING & ANALYTICS
================================================================================
1. Pumunta sa sidebar -> Financial Reports.
2. Income Statement (P&L):
   - Awtomatikong kalkulasyon:
     Gross Profit = Revenue - COGS
     Net Income   = Gross Profit - Operating Expenses
3. Balance Sheet Summary:
   - Kumpletong talaan ng Assets (Cash, Receivables) laban sa Liabilities & Equity (Payables, Capital).
4. AI Financial Insights:
   - Automated financial health rating at predictive insights para sa management.
5. Export to PDF:
   - I-click ang "Export Full Report (PDF)" button para sa formal presentation printout.


================================================================================
📍 9. HAKBANG 8: AUDIT TRAIL (SECURITY, USER ACTORS, AT IP TRACKING)
================================================================================
1. Pumunta sa sidebar -> Audit Trail.
2. Forensic & Compliance Data Table:
   - DATE & TIME: Eksaktong timestamp kung kailan pinroseso ang transaksyon.
   - USER / ACTOR: Kung sino ang nagpasok ng transaksyon (e.g. System Admin,
     E-Commerce Gateway, HR Staff, Supply Chain Agent).
   - IP ADDRESS: Pinagmulang IP address ng kliyente/subsystem (e.g. 192.168.1.45,
     124.106.12.8) para sa security verification.
   - TRANSACTION / EVENT: Transaction code, module type, at operational description.
   - AI AUDIT DECISION: Katayuan ng AI check (PASSED o FLAGGED) kasama ang anomaly score.
3. Walang Monetary Amounts:
   - Ang Audit Trail ay sadyang nakatuon sa SECURITY, AUDITING, at ACCOUNTABILITY
     alinsunod sa standard internal controls (walang money/amount columns upang
     pumukaw sa forensic logging).
